// Private by default

          
Privacy isn't a toggle. It's the architecture.

          
Most "private" AI gateways add tracking on top. InterAPI removes it — no account footprint, untraceable payment, and nothing stored to leak or hand over.

        


        

          

            

              
              
No email to sign up

            

            
Generate a key. That key is the account — no email, no phone, no recovery quiz that links back to a real person.

            
→ identity: none

          

          

            

              
              
Pay in crypto, including Monero

            

            
Top up with BTC, ETH, USDT or XMR. Monero means the payment itself can't be traced back to your key.

            
→ payment: unlinkable

          

          

            

              
              
No logs, no telemetry

            

            
Prompts aren't stored, requests aren't logged, analytics aren't collected. There's nothing to subpoena and nothing to breach.

            
→ retention: zero

          

          

            

              
              
Providers see your prompt, not you

            

            
We strip your identity before forwarding. The model maker receives a request — never a profile, an account, or a billing trail.

            
→ upstream: anonymous

          

        

      

    


    
    

      

        

          
// Works with your stack

          
Point your tools at one URL. Done.

          
InterAPI speaks the OpenAI and Anthropic wire formats, so the agents and CLIs you already use just work — set a base URL and your key.

        


        

          

            
Claude Code anthropic api

            
ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL=https://api.interapi.dev
ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=$INTERAPI_KEY
claude

          

          

            
Codex openai api

            
OPENAI_BASE_URL=https://api.interapi.dev/v1
OPENAI_API_KEY=$INTERAPI_KEY
codex

          

          

            
OpenCode provider gateway

            
provider: openai-compatible
base: https://api.interapi.dev/v1
key:  $INTERAPI_KEY

          

          

            
OpenClaw openai api

            
OPENAI_BASE_URL=https://api.interapi.dev/v1
OPENAI_API_KEY=$INTERAPI_KEY

          

          

            
Gemini CLI custom endpoint

            
GEMINI_API_BASE=https://api.interapi.dev/v1
GEMINI_API_KEY=$INTERAPI_KEY

          

          

            
Anything else raw http

            
curl https://api.interapi.dev/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KEY"

// Pricing

          
Pay per call. No plans, no tiers, no seats.

          
You're not subscribing to software. You're buying tokens of compute, metered in real time, at the model's real rate.

        


        

          

            
01 · Top up

            
Fund with crypto or card

            
Add credit with BTC, ETH, USDT or Monero — or a card if you prefer. Monero keeps the top-up unlinkable from your key.

          

          

            
02 · Use

            
Metered per call

            
Every request is billed at the underlying provider's rate plus a thin margin, debited from your balance in real time as tokens flow.

          

          

            
03 · Never subscribe

            
Zero recurring charges

            
No monthly fee, no minimum, no seat licenses. Credit sits until you spend it. Stop any time — there's nothing to cancel.
